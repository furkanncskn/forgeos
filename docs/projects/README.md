# Enterprise Projects

## Overview

The **Enterprise Projects** department defines how projects are initiated, governed, executed and closed within ForgeOS.

Projects represent the execution layer that transforms approved strategies, product initiatives and architectural decisions into measurable business outcomes.

ForgeOS adopts a **Project as a Product** mindset where every project follows standardized governance, traceability and continuous improvement practices.

---

# Objectives

The Enterprise Projects department aims to:

- Standardize project execution
- Improve delivery predictability
- Increase organizational transparency
- Strengthen governance
- Improve cross-functional collaboration
- Enable AI-assisted project management
- Preserve project knowledge
- Maximize business value

---

# Project Principles

## Business Value First

Every project shall deliver measurable business value aligned with enterprise strategy.

---

## Governance by Design

Projects shall include defined approval gates, quality checkpoints and executive oversight.

---

## Traceability

Every project shall maintain links to:

- Business Objectives
- Requirements
- Decisions
- Architecture
- Workflows
- Risks
- Deliverables
- Lessons Learned

---

## AI Ready

Projects should be structured to support:

- AI planning
- Progress tracking
- Risk prediction
- Documentation generation
- Knowledge retrieval
- Executive reporting

---

## Continuous Learning

Every project contributes to the enterprise knowledge base through retrospectives, decision records and lessons learned.

# Governance

The Enterprise Projects department operates under the authority of the **Project Management Office (PMO)** in collaboration with the **Enterprise Architecture Board**.

Projects affecting multiple business units or enterprise platforms require cross-functional governance and executive approval.

---

# AI Integration

AI capabilities supported across the project lifecycle include:

- Project planning
- Work breakdown generation
- Risk analysis
- Schedule optimization
- Resource recommendations
- Progress summaries
- Executive dashboards
- Lessons learned extraction

AI augments project delivery but does not replace human governance or decision-making.
