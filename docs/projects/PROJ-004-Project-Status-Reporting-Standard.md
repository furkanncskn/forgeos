# Project Status Reporting Standard

---

# Executive Summary

This standard defines how project progress shall be reported across the ForgeOS enterprise.

Consistent reporting enables executives, delivery teams and governance bodies to understand project health, identify risks early and make informed decisions based on objective metrics.

ForgeOS adopts a **single source of project truth**, ensuring all stakeholders receive accurate, timely and standardized project status information.

---

# Purpose

This standard aims to:

- Standardize project reporting
- Improve executive visibility
- Increase delivery transparency
- Enable early risk detection
- Support portfolio governance
- Facilitate AI-assisted reporting

---

# Scope

This standard applies to:

- Product Projects
- Engineering Projects
- AI Projects
- Infrastructure Projects
- Security Projects
- Enterprise Programs
- Internal Initiatives

---

# Reporting Principles

## Accuracy

Reports shall reflect the current state of the project.

---

## Timeliness

Status reports shall be produced according to the defined reporting cadence.

---

## Transparency

Risks, issues and delays shall be reported openly.

---

## Traceability

Reported information shall be traceable to project artifacts and delivery evidence.

---

## Executive Readability

Reports should be concise, measurable and actionable.

---

# Reporting Cadence

| Report Type | Frequency | Audience |
|--------------|-----------|----------|
| Daily Team Update | Daily | Delivery Team |
| Sprint Status | End of Sprint | Engineering & Product |
| Weekly Project Status | Weekly | Project Stakeholders |
| Monthly Executive Report | Monthly | Executive Leadership |
| Portfolio Status | Monthly / Quarterly | PMO & Executives |

---

# Standard Status Levels

| Status | Meaning |
|---------|---------|
| Green | On Track |
| Yellow | At Risk |
| Red | Critical |
| Blue | Completed |
| Grey | On Hold |

---

# Standard Report Structure

Every project status report shall include:

- Executive Summary
- Overall Status
- Milestone Progress
- Scope Status
- Schedule Status
- Budget Status
- Quality Status
- Risks
- Issues
- Decisions Required
- Next Milestones
- Action Items

# Risk Reporting

Every report shall summarize:

- New Risks
- Existing Risks
- Escalated Risks
- Mitigation Progress
- Overall Risk Trend

Risks shall include:

- Probability
- Impact
- Owner
- Target Resolution Date

---

# Issue Reporting

Track:

- Open Issues
- Blockers
- Escalations
- Cross-team Dependencies
- Executive Decisions Needed

---

# Milestone Reporting

For every milestone report:

- Planned Date
- Actual Date
- Status
- Deliverables
- Approval Status

---

# Executive Summary

The first page of every executive report should answer:

- Is the project healthy?
- Are major milestones on track?
- What risks require attention?
- What executive decisions are needed?
- What changed since the last report?

---

# AI Integration

AI may assist with:

- Status summarization
- KPI generation
- Trend analysis
- Risk prediction
- Executive report generation
- Progress forecasting
- Action item extraction
- Portfolio dashboards

Human review is required before reports are distributed externally or to executive leadership.

- Maintain complete traceability
- Be archived for historical analysis
