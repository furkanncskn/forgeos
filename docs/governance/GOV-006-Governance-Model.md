# Governance Model

## Executive Summary

The Governance Model defines how ForgeOS is governed, how authority is delegated, how decisions are approved, and how organizational consistency is maintained.

It establishes a predictable decision-making structure that enables autonomous execution without sacrificing accountability.

This document governs every department, AI Professional, workflow, and organizational asset within ForgeOS.

---

# Purpose

The Governance Model exists to ensure that ForgeOS remains:

- Consistent
- Explainable
- Accountable
- Scalable
- Auditable
- Continuously Improving

Governance provides organizational stability while allowing operational flexibility.

---

# Governance Objectives

ForgeOS governance pursues the following objectives:

- Maintain organizational consistency.
- Clearly define authority.
- Protect company knowledge.
- Standardize decision-making.
- Ensure accountability.
- Enable autonomous execution within controlled boundaries.
- Preserve long-term organizational quality.

---

# Governance Layers

ForgeOS is governed through multiple organizational layers.

```
Human Founder / CEO
        │
        ▼
Governance Office
        │
        ▼
Executive Office
        │
        ▼
Departments
        │
        ▼
AI Professionals
        │
        ▼
Projects
        │
        ▼
Deliverables
```

Each layer has clearly defined authority.

Authority flows downward.

Accountability flows upward.

# Organizational Authority

Authority within ForgeOS is delegated according to responsibility.

| Role | Strategic | Operational | Technical |
|------|-----------|------------|-----------|
| Human Founder | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ |
| CEO Office | ✅ | ✅ | Limited |
| Governance Office | Governance | Governance | No |
| Architecture Office | No | Technical | ✅ |
| Departments | No | Department | Limited |
| AI Professionals | No | Assigned Tasks | No |

# Decision Escalation

When uncertainty exists, decisions escalate upward.

```
AI Professional
        │
Department
        │
Architecture Office
        │
Governance Office
        │
CEO Office
        │
Human Founder
```

Every escalation shall include:

- Problem Statement
- Alternatives
- Recommendation
- Risks
- Supporting Evidence

---

# Governance Lifecycle

Every governance decision follows the same lifecycle.

```
Proposal

↓

Review

↓

Discussion

↓

Approval

↓

Publication

↓

Implementation

↓

Audit

↓

Continuous Improvement
```

No governance decision bypasses this lifecycle.

# Organizational Compliance

Every organizational component shall comply with:

- Constitution
- Governance Documents
- Standards
- Policies
- Approved Decisions

Compliance reviews shall occur periodically.

---

# Governance Reviews

Governance reviews evaluate:

- Organizational consistency
- Policy compliance
- Documentation quality
- Architectural alignment
- Knowledge quality
- Process maturity

Findings become organizational improvement initiatives.

---

# Measuring Governance

ForgeOS measures governance health using indicators such as:

- Documentation coverage
- Decision traceability
- Policy compliance
- Knowledge reuse
- Review completion
- Quality Gate success rate
- Organizational consistency

Metrics evolve through governance decisions.

---

# Governance Boundaries

Governance defines direction.

Departments define execution.

AI Professionals perform work.

Projects produce value.

Responsibilities shall never overlap without explicit approval.
