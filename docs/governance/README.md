# Governance

> Governance is the operating backbone of ForgeOS.

This directory defines **how ForgeOS thinks, makes decisions, governs its AI workforce, and evolves over time.**

Before any AI Agent is created, before any workflow exists, and before any software is built, ForgeOS establishes a governance framework.

Governance is not bureaucracy.

Governance is the system that ensures every decision remains consistent, explainable, auditable, and aligned with the company's mission.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the Governance directory is to define the permanent operating rules of ForgeOS.

These rules are independent of:

- AI model
- Programming language
- Technology stack
- Product domain
- Individual project
- Human contributors

Governance provides stability while allowing continuous evolution.

---

# Objectives

The Governance framework has six primary objectives.

## 1. Establish Organizational Identity

Define what ForgeOS is, why it exists, and how it operates.

---

## 2. Standardize Decision Making

Ensure every strategic and operational decision follows documented principles.

---

## 3. Preserve Organizational Consistency

Guarantee that every project follows the same company standards regardless of the AI model performing the work.

---

## 4. Protect Company Knowledge

Knowledge belongs to ForgeOS.

No individual AI Agent owns knowledge.

Knowledge survives projects, contributors, and technologies.

---

## 5. Enable Continuous Improvement

Governance documents evolve.

Random behavior does not.

Every improvement becomes part of the organization.

---

## 6. Build Trust

ForgeOS must remain understandable by humans.

Every major decision should be explainable.

Every document should be reviewable.

Every process should be auditable.

---

# Governance Philosophy

ForgeOS operates according to one simple belief:

> A great company is built on great systems, not great individuals.

Agents change.

Models change.

Technologies change.

Governance remains.

# Decision Authority

Governance documents define authority.

Authority determines:

- who can decide,
- what may be changed,
- when approval is required,
- how changes become permanent.

No department may override Governance.

No AI Agent may override Governance.

# Amendment Process

Governance documents are intentionally stable.

Changes should occur only when:

- organizational learning requires it,
- inconsistencies are discovered,
- strategic direction changes,
- significant improvements are identified.

Every governance modification must be documented through a Forge Decision Record (FDR).

---

Every governance document follows the same lifecycle.

```
Draft
   │
Architecture Review
   │
Governance Review
   │
Approval
   │
Publication
   │
Continuous Improvement
```

No governance document skips review.

---

# Writing Standards

Every governance document must:

- be written in clear professional English;
- avoid ambiguity;
- remain technology independent whenever possible;
- define responsibilities clearly;
- use consistent terminology;
- reference related documents where appropriate;
- remain understandable by both humans and AI systems.

# ForgeOS Governance Principles

Every governance document must reinforce the following principles.

## Truth Before Confidence

Evidence outweighs confidence.

---

## Systems Before Individuals

ForgeOS is designed around systems rather than people or models.

---

## Documentation First

Knowledge that is not documented does not belong to the organization.

---

## Quality Before Speed

Fast delivery never justifies poor engineering.

---

## Continuous Evolution

ForgeOS evolves through disciplined improvement.

---

## Transparency

Important decisions should always be explainable.

---

## Accountability

Every document has an owner.

Every owner has responsibilities.

Every responsibility has measurable outcomes.
