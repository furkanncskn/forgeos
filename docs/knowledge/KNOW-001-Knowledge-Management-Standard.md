# Knowledge Management Standard

## Executive Summary

Knowledge is a strategic enterprise asset that enables innovation, operational excellence, business continuity and AI-powered decision making.

This standard establishes the governance, lifecycle, ownership and operational practices required to create, maintain, protect and continuously improve enterprise knowledge across the ForgeOS platform.

ForgeOS adopts a **Knowledge-First Enterprise** approach where all organizational knowledge is treated as a governed, searchable and reusable digital asset.

---

# Purpose

This standard aims to:

- Standardize enterprise knowledge management
- Preserve institutional knowledge
- Improve collaboration
- Reduce knowledge silos
- Support AI-powered knowledge retrieval
- Improve documentation quality
- Accelerate onboarding
- Enable continuous organizational learning

---

# Scope

This standard applies to:

- Enterprise Documentation
- Technical Standards
- Architecture Documentation
- Operational Runbooks
- Playbooks
- Knowledge Bases
- Enterprise Wikis
- API Documentation
- AI Knowledge Repositories
- Lessons Learned
- Training Materials

---

# Guiding Principles

## Knowledge as an Asset

Enterprise knowledge shall be managed with the same discipline as software, infrastructure and data.

---

## Single Source of Truth

Every knowledge asset shall have one authoritative source.

Duplicate or conflicting knowledge shall be eliminated.

---

## Knowledge by Default

Every significant project, incident or operational activity shall produce reusable knowledge.

---

## Searchability

Knowledge shall be:

- Indexed
- Searchable
- Tagged
- Categorized
- Discoverable

---

## Continuous Improvement

Knowledge shall evolve continuously through operational feedback and organizational learning.

---

# Knowledge Ownership

Every knowledge asset shall define:

- Knowledge Owner
- Technical Reviewer
- Business Reviewer
- Security Reviewer
- Review Schedule

Ownership shall be reviewed periodically.

---

# Knowledge Governance

Governance shall define:

- Ownership
- Classification
- Approval Workflow
- Review Cycle
- Version Control
- Retention Policy
- Access Policy

Governance shall remain continuously enforced.

---

# Knowledge Repository

Enterprise repositories shall provide:

- Version Control
- Metadata
- Search
- Access Control
- API Access
- Audit Logging
- Workflow Automation

Knowledge repositories shall remain highly available.

---

# Metadata Requirements

Each knowledge asset shall include:

- Identifier
- Title
- Owner
- Category
- Version
- Status
- Classification
- Keywords
- Related Documents
- Last Review Date

Metadata shall support enterprise search and AI indexing.

---

# Knowledge Security

Knowledge platforms shall implement:

- Authentication
- Authorization
- Encryption
- Audit Logging
- Data Classification
- Backup
- Retention Policies

Sensitive knowledge shall be protected according to enterprise security policies.

---

# Knowledge Sharing

Knowledge shall be shared through:

- Enterprise Wiki
- Documentation Portals
- Communities of Practice
- Technical Reviews
- Architecture Reviews
- Training Sessions
- AI Knowledge Platforms

Knowledge sharing shall become part of daily engineering workflows.

# Governance

Enterprise Knowledge Management operates under the authority of the Chief Knowledge Officer.

Changes affecting:

- Enterprise Knowledge Repositories
- Metadata Standards
- Governance Policies
- Knowledge Taxonomy
- AI Knowledge Architecture

shall require approval from:

- Knowledge Management Office
- Enterprise Architecture Board
- Security Office
- AI Governance Committee

Knowledge governance shall be reviewed annually.
