# Knowledge Capture SOP

## Executive Summary

Knowledge Capture ensures that valuable organizational knowledge is systematically preserved instead of remaining with individuals.

This Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) defines how operational, technical and business knowledge shall be collected, reviewed, approved and published throughout the ForgeOS platform.

Knowledge capture is mandatory for projects, incidents, architectural decisions and operational improvements.

---

# Purpose

This SOP aims to:

- Preserve organizational knowledge
- Prevent knowledge loss
- Standardize documentation
- Improve collaboration
- Support AI-powered knowledge platforms
- Improve onboarding
- Accelerate incident resolution
- Enable continuous improvement

---

# Scope

This procedure applies to:

- Software Projects
- Infrastructure Projects
- Security Activities
- Production Incidents
- Architecture Decisions
- Platform Improvements
- Operational Changes
- Major Releases
- Retrospectives
- Research Activities

---

# Guiding Principles

## Capture Early

Knowledge should be documented as work progresses rather than reconstructed later.

---

## Capture Continuously

Knowledge capture is an ongoing activity throughout the project lifecycle.

---

## Knowledge Before Memory

Critical information shall never rely solely on individual memory.

---

## Structured Documentation

Knowledge shall follow approved enterprise templates.

---

## Reusability

Knowledge shall be documented for future reuse by other teams and AI systems.

---

# Knowledge Capture Workflow

```
Work Activity
      ↓
Knowledge Identification
      ↓
Documentation
      ↓
Technical Review
      ↓
Approval
      ↓
Publication
      ↓
Search Indexing
      ↓
Continuous Improvement
```

Each stage shall be traceable.

---

# Capture Triggers

Knowledge shall be captured after:

- New Feature Development
- Production Incident
- Architecture Decision
- Root Cause Analysis
- Security Incident
- Platform Upgrade
- Performance Optimization
- Disaster Recovery Exercise
- Project Retrospective
- Operational Improvement

---

# Knowledge Sources

Knowledge may originate from:

- Engineers
- Architects
- Product Owners
- Security Teams
- Operations Teams
- Customers
- Vendors
- AI Assistants
- Monitoring Platforms

All sources shall be documented.

---

# Required Knowledge Elements

Every captured knowledge asset shall include:

- Title
- Summary
- Business Context
- Technical Details
- Decisions Made
- Risks
- Lessons Learned
- Owner
- Date
- Related Documents

---

# Documentation Templates

Approved templates include:

- Standard Operating Procedure (SOP)
- Runbook
- Playbook
- Architecture Decision Record (ADR)
- Incident Report
- Lessons Learned
- Technical Guide
- Knowledge Article

Templates shall remain standardized.

---

# Review Process

Knowledge shall undergo:

- Technical Review
- Editorial Review
- Security Review (where applicable)
- Business Validation

Critical operational knowledge requires formal approval.

---

# Publication

Approved knowledge shall be:

- Published
- Version Controlled
- Indexed
- Searchable
- Tagged

Publication shall occur in the approved enterprise knowledge repository.

---

# AI Readiness

Captured knowledge shall support:

- Enterprise Search
- Semantic Search
- RAG Pipelines
- AI Assistants
- Enterprise Copilots
- Knowledge Graphs

Content shall be structured for both human and machine consumption.

---

# Metadata Requirements

Every knowledge article shall include:

- Identifier
- Owner
- Category
- Version
- Status
- Classification
- Tags
- Keywords
- Review Date
- Lifecycle State

Metadata shall follow enterprise taxonomy.

---

# Knowledge Validation

Validation shall confirm:

- Technical Accuracy
- Completeness
- Clarity
- Security Compliance
- Business Relevance
- Reusability

Incomplete knowledge shall not be published.

---

# Knowledge Repository

Approved repositories shall provide:

- Version Control
- Search
- Metadata
- Workflow Automation
- Access Control
- API Integration
- Audit Logging

Repositories shall remain continuously available.

# Governance

The Knowledge Capture process operates under the authority of the Chief Knowledge Officer.

Changes affecting:

- Capture Templates
- Review Workflows
- Metadata Standards
- Knowledge Taxonomy

shall require approval from:

- Knowledge Management Office
- Enterprise Architecture Board
- Security Office
- Quality Engineering Office

Knowledge capture compliance shall be audited periodically.
