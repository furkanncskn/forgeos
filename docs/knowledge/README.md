# Knowledge Management Standards

## Overview

The **Knowledge Management Standards** define how enterprise knowledge is created, organized, governed, secured, shared and continuously improved across the ForgeOS platform.

Knowledge is treated as a strategic enterprise asset that enables operational excellence, accelerates onboarding, supports Artificial Intelligence, improves decision making and preserves institutional expertise.

ForgeOS adopts a **Knowledge-as-a-Platform** philosophy where documentation, architecture, operational procedures, lessons learned and AI knowledge bases are maintained as governed, searchable and version-controlled assets.

---

The Knowledge Management Standards aim to:

- Preserve organizational expertise

# Core Principles

## Knowledge as Code

Knowledge shall be version controlled, peer reviewed and managed alongside engineering artifacts.

Documentation shall follow the same governance principles as source code.

---

## Single Source of Truth

Enterprise knowledge shall exist in one authoritative location.

Duplicate or conflicting documentation shall be eliminated.

---

## Search First

Knowledge shall be:

- Searchable
- Tagged
- Categorized
- Indexed
- Discoverable

Enterprise search shall be available across all approved repositories.

---

## Continuous Improvement

Knowledge shall evolve together with:

- Products
- Infrastructure
- Architecture
- Security
- Business Processes

Documentation shall never become static.

---

## AI Ready

Knowledge shall be structured to support:

- Enterprise Search
- Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)
- AI Agents
- Enterprise Copilots
- Knowledge Graphs

# Standards Overview

## KNOW-001 — Knowledge Governance Standard

Defines enterprise governance, ownership, lifecycle and policy requirements for organizational knowledge.

---

## KNOW-002 — Documentation Standard

Defines enterprise documentation structure, templates, metadata, versioning and quality requirements.

---

## KNOW-003 — Knowledge Repository Standard

---

## KNOW-004 — Enterprise Wiki Standard

Defines standards for collaborative knowledge sharing using enterprise wiki platforms.

---

## KNOW-005 — Runbook and Playbook Standard

Defines operational documentation used for recurring operational tasks, incidents and recovery procedures.

---

Defines review cycles, approval workflows, quality assurance and continuous validation of enterprise knowledge.

## KNOW-007 — Lessons Learned Standard

## KNOW-008 — Knowledge Sharing Standard

Defines enterprise search architecture, indexing, metadata, taxonomy and discoverability requirements.

Defines AI-ready knowledge architecture supporting RAG, semantic search, embeddings, vector databases and enterprise AI assistants.

# Governance

Knowledge Management operates under the authority of the Chief Knowledge Officer (CKO) together with Enterprise Architecture and Information Security leadership.

Every knowledge asset shall have:

- An assigned owner
- A defined review schedule
- A classification
- A lifecycle state
- Version history
- Approval records

Knowledge shall remain continuously governed.
