# Knowledge Review SOP

## Executive Summary

Enterprise knowledge remains valuable only when it is accurate, current and trusted.

This Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) defines how documentation, knowledge articles, standards, runbooks and technical content are reviewed, approved and continuously validated across ForgeOS.

Knowledge reviews ensure enterprise documentation remains technically correct, operationally useful, secure and AI-ready.

---

# Purpose

This SOP aims to:

- Standardize knowledge reviews
- Improve documentation quality
- Prevent outdated documentation
- Strengthen governance
- Support compliance
- Improve AI knowledge quality
- Increase organizational trust
- Enable continuous improvement

---

# Scope

This procedure applies to:

- Standards
- SOPs
- Runbooks
- Playbooks
- Technical Guides
- Architecture Documents
- Knowledge Articles
- AI Knowledge Bases
- Operational Procedures
- Lessons Learned

---

# Guiding Principles

## Review Before Publication

No knowledge asset shall be published without completing the required review process.

---

## Continuous Validation

Published knowledge shall be reviewed periodically throughout its lifecycle.

---

## Multi-Disciplinary Review

Reviews shall include technical, security and business perspectives where applicable.

---

## Traceability

Every review decision shall be documented and auditable.

---

## Quality First

Review quality takes precedence over publication speed.

---

# Review Workflow

```
Knowledge Draft
        ↓
Technical Review
        ↓
Security Review
        ↓
Editorial Review
        ↓
Business Approval
        ↓
Publication
        ↓
Periodic Review
```

Every review stage shall produce recorded evidence.

---

# Review Types

## Technical Review

Confirms:

- Technical Accuracy
- Completeness
- Consistency
- Architecture Alignment
- Operational Correctness

---

## Security Review

Confirms:

- Information Classification
- Sensitive Data Protection
- Secure Configuration
- Compliance
- Access Restrictions

---

## Editorial Review

Confirms:

- Readability
- Formatting
- Metadata
- Terminology
- Document Structure

---

## Business Review

Confirms:

- Business Relevance
- Operational Value
- Organizational Alignment
- Ownership

---

# Review Triggers

Knowledge shall be reviewed when:

- Newly Created
- Major Platform Changes
- Architecture Updates
- Security Incidents
- Operational Changes
- Technology Refresh
- Regulatory Changes
- Scheduled Review Date

Reviews shall not be skipped.

---

# Review Criteria

Every review shall evaluate:

- Accuracy
- Completeness
- Security
- Clarity
- Consistency
- Maintainability
- Reusability
- Searchability

Knowledge failing review shall be revised before publication.

---

# Review Frequency

Minimum review frequencies:

| Knowledge Type | Review Interval |
|----------------|-----------------|
| Security Standards | Every 6 Months |
| Architecture Documents | Every 12 Months |
| SOPs | Every 12 Months |
| Runbooks | Every 6 Months |
| Playbooks | Every 6 Months |
| Knowledge Articles | Every 12 Months |
| AI Knowledge Sources | Every 3 Months |

Additional reviews may be triggered by significant organizational changes.

---

# Approval Requirements

Every approved knowledge asset shall define:

- Reviewer
- Approval Date
- Version
- Status
- Next Review Date

Approval records shall remain permanently available.

---

# Review Outcomes

Possible review outcomes include:

- Approved
- Approved with Minor Changes
- Revision Required
- Rejected
- Archived

Review decisions shall include documented justification.

---

# Metadata Validation

Review shall verify:

- Identifier
- Version
- Owner
- Category
- Classification
- Keywords
- Related Standards
- Review Date
- Lifecycle State

Metadata shall comply with enterprise taxonomy.

---

# AI Readiness Review

Knowledge shall be evaluated for:

- Semantic Consistency
- Searchability
- RAG Compatibility
- Chunking Quality
- Metadata Completeness
- Citation Readiness

AI knowledge quality shall be continuously improved.

---

# Documentation Requirements

Review documentation shall include:

- Review Checklist
- Review Comments
- Approval Records
- Revision History
- Security Findings
- Editorial Findings
- Business Approval
- Metadata Validation
- AI Readiness Assessment
- Change History

# Governance

The Knowledge Review process operates under the authority of the Chief Knowledge Officer.

Changes affecting:

- Review Policies
- Approval Workflows
- Review Checklists
- Quality Standards

shall require approval from:

- Knowledge Management Office
- Enterprise Architecture Board
- Security Office
- Quality Engineering Office

Review effectiveness shall be assessed periodically.

# Implementation Notes

ForgeOS recommends implementing review workflows using:

- Azure DevOps
- Git Pull Requests
- GitHub Reviews

- SharePoint
- Microsoft Power Automate

# AI Consumption Notes

AI Review Assistants may support:

- Technical consistency analysis
- Metadata validation
- Grammar and style review
- Duplicate detection
- Security wording analysis
- AI readiness assessment
- Semantic comparison
- Change summarization
- Quality reporting
- Review recommendations
