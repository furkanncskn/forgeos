# Organization

> ForgeOS is designed as a real software company where every role has clear ownership, responsibilities, and accountability.

---

# Purpose

The **Organization** directory defines the organizational structure of ForgeOS.

While the **Governance** directory establishes the rules and the **Company** directory defines the company's identity, the **Organization** directory defines how ForgeOS is structured internally.

It specifies organizational hierarchy, reporting relationships, responsibilities, ownership boundaries, and collaboration rules.

---

# Objectives

The Organization directory exists to:

- Define the organizational hierarchy.
- Establish clear ownership.
- Eliminate responsibility ambiguity.
- Standardize collaboration.
- Support autonomous execution.
- Scale the organization without losing consistency.

---

# Organizational Philosophy

ForgeOS is built around **responsibilities**, not individuals.

People and AI Professionals may change.

Roles remain.

Every responsibility belongs to a role.

Every role belongs to the organization.

---

# Organizational Principles

ForgeOS follows these organizational principles.

## Clear Ownership

Every responsibility has exactly one owner.

Ownership eliminates ambiguity.

---

## Explicit Accountability

Authority always carries accountability.

Responsibilities cannot exist without ownership.

---

## Collaboration Over Isolation

Departments collaborate continuously.

No department operates independently of the organization.

---

## Documentation Over Assumptions

Roles, responsibilities, and organizational decisions must always be documented.

---

## Organizational Scalability

The organization must continue functioning regardless of organizational growth.

Processes should improve as the company expands.

---

# Architect Notes

The Organization directory defines **how ForgeOS is structured**, not how projects are executed.

Operational execution belongs to Departments and Workflows.

---

# Implementation Notes

Every future Department, AI Professional, Team, and Project shall inherit responsibilities from the organizational structure defined here.

Organizational modifications should be introduced through documented governance decisions.
