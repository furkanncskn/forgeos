# Enterprise Playbooks

## Overview

The **Enterprise Playbooks** department contains operational procedures that guide teams through repeatable business, engineering, security and operational activities.

Unlike standards, which define *what* must be followed, playbooks describe *how* teams execute specific scenarios consistently across the ForgeOS platform.

Playbooks are designed for both human operators and AI Agents, providing structured, repeatable workflows for normal operations, incident response and continuous improvement.

---

# Objectives

The Enterprise Playbooks aim to:

- Standardize operational execution
- Improve incident response
- Reduce operational variability
- Accelerate recovery
- Increase automation
- Improve cross-team collaboration
- Support AI-assisted operations
- Preserve operational knowledge

# Core Principles

## Operational Consistency

Every recurring operational activity shall follow an approved playbook.

---

## Automation First

Operational tasks should be automated wherever practical.

Human intervention should focus on decision making rather than repetitive execution.

---

## Evidence Driven

Every execution shall generate logs, metrics and operational evidence.

---

## Continuous Improvement

- Incidents
- Retrospectives
- Platform Changes
- Security Findings
- Lessons Learned

## AI Ready

Playbooks shall be structured for execution by:

- AI Agents
- Enterprise Copilots
- Automation Platforms
- Workflow Engines

# Playbook Overview

## PB-001 — Incident Response Playbook

Provides standardized operational procedures for detecting, triaging, investigating and resolving production incidents.

---

## PB-002 — Production Deployment Playbook

Defines the operational workflow for safely deploying applications, infrastructure and AI systems into production.

---

## PB-003 — Disaster Recovery Playbook

Describes recovery procedures following infrastructure failures, regional outages or catastrophic events.

---

## PB-004 — Security Incident Playbook

Provides operational guidance for cybersecurity incidents including containment, eradication, recovery and post-incident analysis.

---

## PB-005 — Service Onboarding Playbook

Defines how new applications, services and infrastructure components are introduced into the ForgeOS platform.

---

## PB-006 — Change Management Playbook

Defines operational procedures for planning, approving, implementing and validating enterprise changes.

---

## PB-007 — Release Management Playbook

Standardizes software release preparation, approval, deployment and post-release validation.

---

## PB-008 — AI Agent Operations Playbook

Defines operational workflows for deploying, governing and monitoring enterprise AI Agents.

---

## PB-009 — Platform Maintenance Playbook

Provides repeatable procedures for routine maintenance including upgrades, patching, backups and health verification.

---

## PB-010 — Business Continuity Playbook

Defines operational procedures for maintaining essential business services during major disruptions.

# Governance

Enterprise Playbooks operate under the authority of the **Operations Governance Board**.

Each playbook shall define:

- Owner
- Reviewers
- Trigger Conditions
- Inputs
- Outputs
- Execution Steps
- Success Criteria
- Escalation Path
- Review Schedule
- Version History

Playbooks shall be reviewed after significant operational events and at least annually.

---
