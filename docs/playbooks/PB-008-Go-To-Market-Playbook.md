# Go-To-Market Playbook

## Executive Summary

This playbook defines the standardized process for successfully launching a product into the market and scaling customer acquisition.

It aligns product, marketing, sales, customer success and operations to ensure a coordinated product launch with measurable business outcomes.

ForgeOS adopts a **Market Validation → Launch → Scale** approach where continuous customer feedback drives product growth.

---

# Purpose

This playbook aims to:

- Standardize product launches
- Accelerate customer acquisition
- Improve cross-functional alignment
- Maximize launch success
- Increase product adoption
- Reduce market risk
- Support data-driven decisions
- Enable AI-assisted GTM execution

---

# Scope

This playbook applies to:

- SaaS Products
- AI Products
- Mobile Applications
- Enterprise Platforms
- APIs
- Digital Services
- Internal Product Commercialization
- Strategic Product Launches

---

# Trigger

Execute this playbook when:

- An MVP is approved for public release
- A major product version is launched
- A new market is entered
- A strategic product initiative begins

Required inputs include:

# Go-To-Market Lifecycle

```
Market Research
        ↓
Positioning
        ↓
Pricing
        ↓
Launch Planning
        ↓
Sales Enablement
        ↓
Marketing Campaign
        ↓
Public Launch
        ↓
Customer Adoption
        ↓
Optimization
```

Each phase shall include measurable deliverables.

---

# Phase 1 — Market Research

Activities:

- Identify target market
- Analyze competitors
- Validate customer needs
- Estimate market size
- Define ideal customer profile (ICP)

Deliverables:

- Market Analysis
- Customer Personas
- Competitive Report

---

# Phase 2 — Positioning

Activities:

- Define unique value proposition
- Create messaging framework
- Differentiate from competitors
- Develop product narrative

Deliverables:

- Positioning Statement
- Messaging Guide

---

# Phase 3 — Pricing Strategy

Activities:

- Evaluate pricing models
- Analyze competitors
- Estimate profitability
- Validate willingness to pay

Common pricing models:

- Subscription
- Usage-Based
- Freemium
- Enterprise Licensing

Deliverables:

- Pricing Model
- Commercial Policy

---

# Phase 4 — Launch Planning

Activities:

- Define launch milestones
- Coordinate stakeholders
- Prepare communication plan
- Finalize operational readiness

Deliverables:

- Launch Plan
- Launch Checklist

---

# Phase 5 — Sales Enablement

Activities:

- Train sales teams
- Prepare product demos
- Develop sales collateral
- Build objection handling guides

Deliverables:

- Sales Playbook
- Demo Environment
- FAQ

---

# Phase 6 — Marketing Campaign

Activities:

- Website updates
- Content marketing
- Social media campaigns
- Email campaigns
- Public relations
- Webinar planning

Deliverables:

- Marketing Campaign
- Content Calendar
- Campaign Analytics

---

# Phase 7 — Public Launch

Activities:

- Publish product
- Activate campaigns
- Monitor customer activity
- Collect launch metrics
- Support early adopters

Deliverables:

- Launch Report
- Adoption Dashboard

---

# Phase 8 — Customer Adoption

Activities:

- Customer onboarding
- Product education
- Support engagement
- Collect customer feedback
- Measure satisfaction

Deliverables:

- Adoption Metrics
- Customer Health Dashboard

---

# Phase 9 — Continuous Optimization

Activities:

- Analyze KPIs
- Optimize messaging
- Improve onboarding
- Adjust pricing
- Prioritize roadmap improvements

Deliverables:

- Growth Plan
- Product Improvement Backlog

---
