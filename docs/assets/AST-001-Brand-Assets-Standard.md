# Brand Assets Standard

---

# Executive Summary

This standard defines how brand assets are created, managed, versioned, distributed and governed across the ForgeOS ecosystem.

Brand assets represent the visual identity of the organization and shall remain consistent across products, documentation, marketing materials and digital experiences.

ForgeOS adopts a **Single Source of Brand Truth** approach where all approved brand assets are centrally managed.

---

# Purpose

This standard aims to:

- Ensure brand consistency
- Standardize asset usage
- Prevent unauthorized modifications
- Improve discoverability
- Support enterprise design systems
- Enable reusable branding assets

---

# Scope

This standard applies to:

- Logos
- Logomarks
- Brand Colors
- Typography
- Icons
- Illustrations
- Social Media Assets
- Presentation Templates
- Marketing Graphics
- Favicons
- Application Icons

---

# Brand Principles

## Consistency

Brand assets shall be used consistently across all enterprise products.

---

## Authenticity

Only officially approved assets may be used.

---

## Reusability

Assets should be reusable across teams and platforms.

---

## Accessibility

Brand assets shall comply with accessibility requirements where applicable.

---

## Version Control

Every published asset shall maintain version history.

---

# Asset Categories

## Primary Logo

Official enterprise logo.

---

## Secondary Logos

Approved logo variations.

---

## Monochrome Logos

Single-color variants.

---

## Icon Assets

Application icons, favicons and system icons.

---

## Brand Colors

Official color palette.

Each color shall define:

- HEX
- RGB
- CMYK
- Usage Guidelines

---

## Typography

Define:

- Primary Typeface
- Secondary Typeface
- Fallback Fonts
- Usage Rules

---

## Illustration Library

Reusable enterprise illustrations.

---

# Naming Convention

Asset names should follow:

```text
brand-category-name-version.extension
```

Example:

```text
forgeos-logo-primary-v1.svg
forgeos-icon-dark-v2.png
```

---

# File Formats

Preferred formats:

Vector:

- SVG
- PDF
- AI

Raster:

- PNG
- WebP

Print:

- PDF
- EPS

Avoid distributing editable working files unless explicitly authorized.

---

# Metadata Requirements

Every asset shall include:

- Asset ID
- Asset Name
- Description
- Owner
- Category
- Version
- Status
- Tags
- License
- Last Updated

---

# Storage Requirements

Brand assets shall be stored within the centralized enterprise asset repository.

Repositories shall support:

- Versioning
- Search
- Preview
- Access Control
- Audit History

---

# Usage Guidelines

Brand assets shall define:

- Minimum Size
- Clear Space
- Color Variants
- Background Rules
- Incorrect Usage Examples
- Accessibility Notes

---

# Quality Requirements

Brand assets shall be:

- High Resolution
- Vector-based where possible
- Accessible
- Optimized for digital and print
- Consistent with brand identity
- Properly documented

# Governance

The Brand Assets Standard is governed by the Brand & Design Office.

All enterprise brand assets shall:

- Be centrally managed
- Maintain version history
- Follow naming standards
- Preserve brand consistency
- Undergo formal approval
- Support enterprise-wide reuse
