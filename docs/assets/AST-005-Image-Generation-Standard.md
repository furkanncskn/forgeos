# Image Generation Standard

---

# Executive Summary

This standard defines how AI-generated images are created, reviewed, approved and managed across the ForgeOS ecosystem.

AI image generation enables rapid content creation while ensuring consistency with enterprise branding, legal requirements and Responsible AI principles.

ForgeOS adopts a **Human Approved AI Generated Asset** model.

---

# Purpose

This standard aims to:

- Standardize AI image generation
- Ensure visual consistency
- Protect brand identity
- Reduce legal risks
- Support Responsible AI
- Improve design productivity

---

# Scope

This standard applies to:

- Marketing Images
- Product Graphics
- Illustrations
- Concept Art
- Social Media Assets
- Presentation Graphics
- Blog Images
- Technical Illustrations
- UI Mockups
- Educational Content

---

# Image Generation Principles

## Human Review

Every production image generated by AI shall be reviewed by a qualified human before publication.

---

## Brand Consistency

Generated images shall align with enterprise branding, tone and design language.

---

## Transparency

AI-generated assets shall be identifiable within internal asset metadata.

---

## Originality

Generated images should avoid unnecessary similarity to existing copyrighted works.

---

## Accessibility

Generated images shall support accessibility requirements, including descriptive alternative text where appropriate.

---

# Approved Use Cases

AI image generation is recommended for:

- Concept exploration
- Moodboards
- Storyboards
- Marketing drafts
- Documentation graphics
- Internal presentations
- Educational illustrations
- Product ideation

---

# Restricted Use Cases

Additional approval is required for:

- Corporate identity assets
- Official logos
- Legal documents
- Investor communications
- Executive portraits
- Regulatory submissions
- Customer contractual materials

---

# Prohibited Use Cases

AI-generated images shall not be used to:

- Misrepresent real events
- Create deceptive content
- Infringe copyrights
- Circumvent licensing
- Produce offensive or discriminatory material
- Falsify evidence or documentation

---

# Prompt Design Requirements

Image prompts should define:

- Business Objective
- Subject
- Style
- Composition
- Color Palette
- Brand References
- Aspect Ratio
- Resolution
- Output Format
- Intended Usage

---

# Image Quality Requirements

Production assets shall meet:

- High Resolution
- Visual Consistency
- Correct Anatomy (where applicable)
- Proper Typography
- Brand Compliance
- Artifact-Free Rendering
- Accessibility Considerations

---

# Metadata Requirements

Every generated image shall include:

- Asset ID
- Prompt Version
- Generation Date
- Creator
- AI Model
- Human Reviewer
- Version
- Status
- Intended Usage
- License Information

---

# File Formats

Preferred delivery formats:

Primary:

- PNG
- SVG (when applicable)
- WebP

Editable sources:

- Layered design files
- Prompt history
- Generation metadata

---

# Review Workflow

```text
Prompt Creation
      │
      ▼
AI Image Generation
      │
      ▼
Quality Review
      │
      ▼
Brand Review
      │
      ▼
Legal Review (if required)
      │
      ▼
Publication
```

---

# Version Control

Every generated asset shall maintain:

- Prompt history
- Image versions
- Review history
- Approval history
- Change log

Regeneration events shall be documented.

---

# Security Requirements

Generated assets shall:

- Avoid embedding sensitive information
- Protect confidential designs
- Respect access controls
- Comply with enterprise data classification
- Follow approved AI platform policies

---

# AI Integration

Approved AI platforms may assist with:

- Image generation
- Illustration creation
- Background replacement
- Style exploration
- Layout concepts
- Color variations
- Asset enhancement

Human approval remains mandatory for production use.

# Governance

The Image Generation Standard is governed by the Brand & Design Office in collaboration with the AI Governance Office.

All AI-generated images shall:

- Preserve generation metadata
- Follow Responsible AI principles
- Undergo human review
- Maintain version history
- Comply with branding guidelines
- Respect legal and licensing requirements
