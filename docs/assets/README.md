# Enterprise Assets

## Overview

The **Enterprise Assets** department defines the governance, organization and lifecycle of reusable digital assets used throughout the ForgeOS ecosystem.

Assets include visual resources, documentation, templates, diagrams, design system components, icons, media, code snippets and other reusable artifacts that support consistent product development.

ForgeOS adopts an **Asset-as-a-Platform** approach where enterprise assets are centrally managed, version-controlled and reusable across projects.

---

# Objectives

The Enterprise Assets department aims to:

- Standardize reusable assets
- Improve organizational consistency
- Reduce duplicated work
- Increase design and engineering productivity
- Strengthen brand consistency
- Improve discoverability
- Enable asset governance
- Support enterprise knowledge reuse

# Asset Principles

## Reusable

Assets should be designed for reuse across multiple teams and projects.

---

## Version Controlled

Every asset shall maintain version history and change tracking.

---

## Discoverable

Assets shall include metadata, tagging and classification to enable efficient discovery.

---

## Governed

Assets shall follow enterprise governance, security, licensing and branding policies.

---

## High Quality

Only reviewed and approved assets shall be published to the enterprise repository.

# Versioning

Enterprise assets follow Semantic Versioning where applicable:

```text

```

Breaking changes shall be documented and communicated to asset consumers.
