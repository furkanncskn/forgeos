# Illustration Standard

---

# Executive Summary

This standard defines how illustrations are designed, organized, versioned and used throughout the ForgeOS ecosystem.

Illustrations enhance user experience by simplifying complex concepts, strengthening brand identity and improving communication across products, documentation and marketing materials.

ForgeOS adopts a **Unified Illustration System** to ensure visual consistency and long-term maintainability.

---

# Purpose

This standard aims to:

- Standardize illustration styles
- Improve visual consistency
- Support reusable assets
- Strengthen enterprise branding
- Enhance user experience
- Reduce duplicated artwork

---

# Scope

This standard applies to:

- Product Illustrations
- Empty States
- Onboarding Graphics
- Documentation Graphics
- Marketing Illustrations
- Landing Pages
- Educational Content
- Infographics
- Process Illustrations
- Technical Illustrations

---

# Illustration Principles

## Simplicity

Illustrations shall communicate concepts clearly without unnecessary visual complexity.

---

## Consistency

All illustrations shall follow the same:

- Visual style
- Perspective
- Color palette
- Line weight
- Corner treatment
- Composition rules

---

## Reusability

Illustrations should be modular and reusable across products and documentation.

---

## Accessibility

Illustrations shall support accessibility by complementing—not replacing—textual information.

---

## Brand Alignment

Illustrations shall reinforce enterprise visual identity.

# Design Style

Recommended style:

- Flat Illustration
- Soft Geometric Shapes
- Rounded Corners
- Minimal Detail
- Friendly Appearance
- Consistent Perspective

Avoid excessive realism unless specifically required.

---

# Color Guidelines

Illustrations shall use:

- Brand Color Palette
- Semantic Colors
- Design Tokens
- Accessible Contrast Ratios

Decorative colors should remain consistent with enterprise branding.

---

# Composition Rules

Illustrations should:

- Maintain balanced spacing
- Avoid unnecessary clutter
- Scale proportionally
- Support responsive layouts
- Preserve readability at multiple sizes

---

# File Formats

Preferred formats:

Primary:

- SVG

Secondary:

- PNG
- WebP

Editable Sources:

- Figma
- SVG
- AI

Raster formats shall not be considered the master source.

---

# Naming Convention

Illustration assets shall follow:

```text
category-topic-style-version.extension
```

Examples:

```text
empty-state-no-data-flat-v1.svg

marketing-cloud-platform-v2.svg

technical-api-flow-v1.svg
```

---

# Metadata Requirements

Every illustration shall include:

- Illustration ID
- Name
- Description
- Category
- Style
- Version
- Designer
- Status
- Keywords
- Last Updated

---

# Accessibility Requirements

Illustrations shall:

- Support accompanying text
- Maintain sufficient contrast
- Avoid conveying meaning through color alone
- Include alternative text where applicable
- Avoid distracting animation unless required

---

# Quality Requirements

Illustrations shall be:

- Vector based
- Responsive
- High resolution
- Brand compliant
- Well documented
- Easily reusable

- Follow approved illustration styles
- Maintain metadata
- Preserve version history
- Support accessibility
- Be centrally managed
- Undergo design review before publication
