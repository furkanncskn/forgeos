## What is ForgeOS?

ForgeOS is an AI-native Company Operating System designed to transform software development from isolated AI interactions into structured organizational collaboration.

Instead of relying on a single AI assistant, ForgeOS organizes specialized AI professionals into departments that follow governance, standards, workflows, documentation, and quality systems similar to world-class software companies.

ForgeOS is model-independent.

Claude, GPT, Gemini, Grok, DeepSeek, or future language models are workers inside the company—not the company itself.

---

## Why ForgeOS Exists

Modern AI tools generate code.

ForgeOS builds organizations.

Most AI systems focus on completing individual tasks.

ForgeOS focuses on creating a complete software delivery organization capable of researching, designing, engineering, testing, releasing, and continuously improving digital products.
