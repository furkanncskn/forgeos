# Product Decision Log

---

# Executive Summary

The Product Decision Log is the official repository for documenting strategic and tactical product decisions made throughout the product lifecycle.

It records why product decisions were made, what alternatives were considered, who approved them and how they affect customers, engineering and business outcomes.

The Product Decision Log ensures every major product decision remains transparent, traceable and reusable across future initiatives.

---

# Purpose

This document aims to:

- Preserve product knowledge
- Improve roadmap transparency
- Document product strategy
- Support executive governance
- Enable data-driven product evolution
- Reduce repeated product discussions

---

This log applies to decisions involving:

- Product Vision

# Decision Traceability

Product decisions shall reference:

- Product Vision
- Market Research
- User Research
- PRDs
- Roadmaps
- Backlog Items
- UX Research
- Customer Feedback
- Analytics

Implementation artifacts shall reference the originating product decision.

# Governance

The Product Decision Log is governed by the Product Management Office.

Approved product decisions become authoritative inputs for:

- Product Roadmaps
- Product Backlogs
- Engineering Planning
- Release Planning
- Portfolio Reviews

All strategic product decisions shall be retained as organizational knowledge.
