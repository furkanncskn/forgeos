# Business Decision Log

---

# Executive Summary

The Business Decision Log is the official repository for documenting strategic business decisions that influence the organization's direction, investments and operational priorities.

It provides a permanent record of executive decisions, their supporting rationale, expected outcomes and measurable business impact.

This log enables transparent governance while preserving institutional knowledge for future leadership teams.

---

# Purpose

This document aims to:

- Preserve strategic business knowledge
- Improve executive transparency
- Support corporate governance
- Record investment decisions
- Enable organizational learning
- Improve long-term decision consistency

---

This log applies to decisions involving:

- Corporate Strategy
- Business Model

# Evaluation Criteria

Business decisions should consider:

- Strategic Alignment
- Customer Value
- Revenue Potential
- Cost

- Competitive Position

- Long-Term Sustainability

# Decision Status

Supported states:

- Proposed
- Business Review
- Executive Review
- Approved
- Implemented
- Superseded
- Cancelled
- Rejected

Decision history shall remain permanently accessible.

---

# Decision Traceability

Business decisions shall reference:

- Strategic Plans
- Business Cases
- Market Research
- Financial Analysis
- Product Roadmaps
- Investment Portfolios
- Enterprise KPIs
- Governance Records

Operational execution artifacts shall reference the originating business decision where applicable.

---

# Executive Review Process

Every strategic business decision shall undergo:

1. Strategy Review
2. Financial Review
3. Product & Operations Review
4. Risk Assessment
5. Executive Leadership Approval

Major investments may require Board approval according to corporate governance policies.

---

# Business Decision Quality Principles

Every business decision should be:

- Strategically Aligned
- Evidence Based
- Financially Justified
- Measurable
- Sustainable
- Transparent
- Risk Aware
- Traceable

# Governance

The Business Decision Log is governed by the Executive Strategy Office.

Approved business decisions become authoritative references for:

- Corporate Strategy
- Product Portfolio
- Investment Planning
- Organizational Planning
- Enterprise Roadmaps
- Operational Execution

All strategic decisions shall be retained as part of the organization's permanent knowledge repository.
