# Research to Product Workflow

---

# Executive Summary

This workflow defines how validated research outcomes are transformed into an approved product initiative.

It ensures that research findings become structured product requirements, prioritized roadmaps and implementation-ready backlogs before engineering begins.

ForgeOS follows an **Evidence → Strategy → Execution** approach where product investments are justified through validated research and measurable business objectives.

---

# Purpose

This workflow aims to:

- Convert research into product strategy
- Standardize product planning
- Prioritize customer value
- Reduce delivery uncertainty
- Align business and engineering
- Produce implementation-ready documentation

---

# Trigger

This workflow starts when:

- Market research is approved
- User research is completed
- Executive sponsor approves opportunity
- Innovation committee accepts proposal

---

# Inputs

Required inputs:

- Market Research Report
- User Research Report
- Competitor Analysis
- Business Opportunity Assessment
- Executive Recommendations

---

# Outputs

Successful completion produces:

- Product Vision
- Product Requirements Document (PRD)
- Product Roadmap
- Product Backlog
- High-Level Solution Proposal
- Executive Go Decision

---

# Phase 1 — Opportunity Assessment

Activities:

- Validate research findings
- Estimate business value
- Review customer demand
- Assess market timing
- Estimate ROI

Deliverables:

- Opportunity Assessment
- Investment Recommendation

---

# Phase 2 — Product Vision

Activities:

- Define product mission
- Identify target users
- Define success metrics
- Establish strategic goals

Deliverables:

- Product Vision
- Product Objectives

---

# Phase 3 — PRD Development

Activities:

- Document business requirements
- Define functional requirements
- Define non-functional requirements
- Establish acceptance criteria
- Define MVP scope

Deliverables:

- Product Requirements Document

---

# Phase 4 — Product Roadmap

Activities:

- Define releases
- Prioritize initiatives
- Allocate resources
- Identify dependencies
- Plan milestones

Deliverables:

- Product Roadmap

---

# Phase 5 — Backlog Creation

Activities:

- Create epics
- Define features
- Write user stories
- Estimate effort
- Prioritize backlog

Deliverables:

- Product Backlog

---

# Phase 6 — Architecture Review

Activities:

- Technical feasibility review
- Platform compatibility
- Security review
- Data architecture review
- Operational readiness assessment

Deliverables:

- Solution Architecture
- Architecture Decision Records (ADRs)

---

# Phase 7 — Executive Approval

Review includes:

- Business viability
- Technical feasibility
- Financial investment
- Delivery capacity
- Strategic alignment

Possible outcomes:

- Approved
- Rework Required
- Deferred
- Rejected
