# Idea to Research Workflow

---

# Executive Summary

This workflow standardizes how a raw idea becomes a validated business opportunity through structured research, user discovery and market analysis.

The objective is to ensure that product investments are based on evidence rather than assumptions.

---

# Purpose

This workflow enables the organization to:

- Capture ideas consistently
- Evaluate strategic alignment
- Validate customer problems
- Analyze competitors
- Reduce investment risk
- Produce research-driven product opportunities

---

# Trigger

Workflow starts when:

- New product idea submitted
- Customer request received
- Innovation initiative begins
- Executive proposal received
- Market opportunity identified

---

# Inputs

Required inputs:

- Idea description
- Business objective
- Problem statement
- Initial assumptions
- Stakeholder information

---

# Outputs

Successful completion produces:

- Product Discovery Report
- Market Research
- User Research
- Competitor Analysis
- Product Recommendation
- Executive Go / No-Go Decision

---

---

# Phase 1 — Idea Registration

Activities

- Register idea
- Assign identifier
- Record submitter
- Capture objectives
- Notify Product Office

Deliverables

- Idea Record

---

# Phase 2 — Initial Screening

Evaluate:

- Business relevance
- Strategic alignment
- Duplicate ideas
- Initial feasibility

Possible outcomes:

- Proceed
- Merge
- Reject
- Hold

---

# Phase 3 — Business Alignment

Validate alignment with:

- Company strategy
- Product portfolio
- Revenue goals
- Technology roadmap

Deliverable:

- Business Alignment Report

---

# Phase 4 — Market Research

Activities

- Estimate TAM/SAM/SOM
- Identify trends
- Analyze competitors
- Evaluate pricing
- Review regulations

Deliverable:

- Market Research Report

---

# Phase 5 — User Research

Activities

- User interviews
- Surveys
- Persona creation
- Journey mapping
- Pain point analysis

Deliverable:

- User Research Report

---

# Phase 6 — Competitor Analysis

Evaluate:

- Feature comparison
- Pricing
- Positioning
- Technology
- Market differentiation

Deliverable:

- Competitor Analysis Report

---

# Phase 7 — Research Review

Cross-functional review involving:

- Product
- UX
- Marketing
- Architecture
- Engineering

Review topics:

- Evidence quality
- Opportunity size
- Risks
- Strategic fit

---

# Phase 8 — Decision

Possible outcomes

## Go

Proceed to Product Discovery / PRD.

---

## Validate Further

Additional research required.

---

## Hold

Idea remains in innovation backlog.

---

## Reject

Archive with documented rationale.
