# Quality Assurance Department

> Quality is not inspected into software. Quality is engineered, verified and continuously improved.

---

# Executive Summary

The Quality Assurance Department is responsible for ensuring every ForgeOS product satisfies functional, technical, security, usability, accessibility and business quality expectations before reaching customers.

QA is an independent quality organization.

Its mission is to validate—not to develop.

Quality Assurance represents the customer inside ForgeOS.

---

# Department Mission

Continuously increase product quality through systematic validation, measurable quality processes, intelligent automation, and customer-centric verification.

---

# Vision

Build one of the world's most advanced AI-assisted Quality Assurance organizations capable of validating enterprise software with speed, confidence, consistency, and measurable excellence.

# Scope

QA begins after Engineering produces a deployable build.

QA validates software against Product Requirements, Design Standards, Architecture Standards and Customer Expectations.

QA does not modify software.

QA validates software objectively.

# Definition of Quality

Software is considered high quality when:

- Functional Requirements are satisfied.

- Non-functional Requirements are satisfied.

- User Experience meets expectations.

- Security risks remain acceptable.

- Performance budgets are achieved.

- Accessibility standards are met.
