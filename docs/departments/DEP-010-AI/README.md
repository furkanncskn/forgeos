# DEP-010 — Artificial Intelligence

## Overview

The **Artificial Intelligence Department** defines the enterprise architecture, governance, engineering standards and operational practices for designing, developing, deploying and operating AI capabilities across the ForgeOS platform.

Artificial Intelligence is treated as a strategic enterprise capability rather than an isolated technology. Every AI solution shall be governed using standardized engineering, security, ethical and operational principles to ensure reliable, explainable and secure AI adoption.

The standards within this department establish a common framework for Generative AI, Machine Learning, AI Agents, Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), MLOps and Enterprise AI Governance.

---

# Objectives

The Artificial Intelligence Department aims to:

- Standardize enterprise AI architecture

- Enable enterprise-wide AI adoption

# Core Principles

## AI by Design

Artificial Intelligence shall be considered during architecture and product planning rather than introduced as an afterthought.

---

## Human Oversight

Humans remain accountable for all business-critical AI decisions.

AI systems shall augment—not replace—human decision making where organizational or regulatory risks exist.

---

## Responsible AI

Every AI solution shall demonstrate:

- Fairness
- Transparency
- Accountability
- Privacy
- Security
- Reliability
- Explainability

---

## Security by Default

AI systems shall comply with enterprise:

- Security
- Identity
- Compliance
- Privacy
- Governance

---

## Continuous Learning

AI capabilities shall evolve through:

- Monitoring
- Feedback
- Evaluation
- Retraining
- Governance Reviews

---

## Automation First

AI engineering workflows should maximize automation while maintaining governance and human approval for production changes.

## AI-004 — Prompt Engineering Standard

Defines prompt lifecycle management, prompt versioning, prompt testing and optimization.

---

## AI-006 — AI Agent Standard

Defines autonomous AI agents, orchestration, tool usage, approval workflows and operational controls.

---

## AI-007 — MLOps Standard

Defines model training, deployment, CI/CD, model registry, feature stores and production operations.

---

## AI-008 — Model Lifecycle Management Standard

Defines governance from model development through retirement including versioning, validation and approvals.

---

## AI-009 — AI Evaluation and Benchmark Standard

Defines evaluation methodologies, benchmark datasets, regression testing and quality metrics.

## AI-010 — Responsible AI Standard

---

## AI-011 — AI Observability Standard

Defines monitoring, tracing, token analytics, latency, hallucination detection and AI telemetry.

---

## AI-012 — AI Operations Standard

Defines operational procedures for AI production environments, support, scaling and incident management.

---

## AI-013 — AI Risk Management Standard

Defines AI-specific risk identification, assessment, mitigation and governance aligned with enterprise risk frameworks.

---

## AI-014 — AI Development Workflow SOP

Defines the end-to-end engineering workflow for AI product development including experimentation, validation, deployment and operational handover.

## AI-015 — AI Innovation and Research Standard

Defines research governance, experimentation processes, prototype evaluation and enterprise AI innovation management.

# Governance

The Artificial Intelligence Department operates under the authority of the Chief AI Officer (CAIO), together with the Chief Technology Officer (CTO), Chief Information Security Officer (CISO) and Chief Data Officer (CDO).

Major architectural decisions, production AI deployments and enterprise AI policies shall be reviewed through the AI Governance Committee.
