# AI Governance Standard

## Executive Summary

Artificial Intelligence must operate under clear governance, accountability and risk management to ensure that AI systems remain secure, ethical, transparent and aligned with organizational objectives.

This standard establishes the enterprise governance framework for Artificial Intelligence across ForgeOS.

It defines organizational responsibilities, approval workflows, oversight mechanisms, risk management processes and operational controls governing AI throughout its lifecycle.

ForgeOS adopts a **Responsible AI Governance** model where all production AI capabilities remain explainable, auditable and continuously supervised.

---

# Purpose

This standard aims to:

- Establish enterprise AI governance
- Define AI ownership
- Strengthen accountability
- Govern AI risk
- Support regulatory compliance
- Standardize AI decision making
- Improve AI transparency
- Enable responsible AI adoption

# Guiding Principles

## Human Accountability

Humans remain accountable for every production AI system.

Responsibility shall never be delegated to AI.

---

## Transparency

AI capabilities shall clearly communicate:

- Purpose
- Limitations
- Data Sources
- Ownership
- Decision Scope

---

## Responsible AI

Every AI solution shall demonstrate:

- Fairness
- Privacy
- Security
- Explainability
- Reliability
- Accountability

---

## Governance by Design

Governance shall be embedded into every stage of the AI lifecycle.

---

## Continuous Oversight

Governance shall continue after production deployment through continuous monitoring and review.

# AI Classification

Enterprise AI systems shall be classified.

| Classification | Description |
|---------------|-------------|
| Experimental | Research and Proof of Concept |
| Internal | Internal productivity solutions |
| Business Critical | Operational decision support |
| Regulated | Compliance or legal impact |
| High Risk | Financial, healthcare or safety impact |

Classification determines governance requirements.

# Risk Assessment

Every AI initiative shall assess:

- Business Risk
- Security Risk
- Privacy Risk
- Ethical Risk
- Operational Risk
- Regulatory Risk
- Vendor Risk
- Model Risk

Risk assessments shall be documented.

---

# Approval Requirements

Production AI systems require approval from:

- Business Owner
- AI Engineering Lead
- Security Office
- Enterprise Architecture
- AI Governance Committee (where applicable)

High-risk systems require executive approval.

---

# Responsible AI Requirements

Production AI shall demonstrate:

- Explainability
- Human Oversight
- Bias Evaluation
- Auditability
- Traceability
- Security
- Privacy Protection

Responsible AI reviews shall be documented.

---

# Vendor Governance

Third-party AI providers shall be evaluated for:

- Security
- Compliance
- Privacy
- Availability
- Licensing
- Operational Risk
- Data Residency

Vendor approval shall be documented.

---

# Regulatory Compliance

AI systems shall comply with applicable regulations including:

- GDPR

- ISO/IEC 23894

# Governance

Enterprise AI Governance operates under the authority of the Chief AI Officer.

The AI Governance Committee shall review:

- High-Risk AI Systems
- Enterprise AI Strategy
- Governance Policies
- AI Investments
- Production AI Exceptions

Policy exceptions shall include documented business justification, risk acceptance and expiration dates.
