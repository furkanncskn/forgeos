# Competitor Analysis Standard Operating Procedure (SOP)

## Executive Summary

This Standard Operating Procedure defines how ForgeOS performs competitor analysis before initiating any product, feature, or strategic business decision.

The objective is not to copy competitors.

The objective is to understand the market well enough to build a better solution.

Competitive intelligence is an organizational asset.

---

# Purpose

This SOP standardizes competitor research to ensure every product initiative is supported by objective market intelligence.

The analysis identifies:

- Existing solutions
- Market gaps
- Competitive advantages
- Customer expectations
- Differentiation opportunities

---

# Scope

This SOP applies to:

- New Products
- SaaS Platforms
- Mobile Applications
- AI Products
- Product Features
- Platform Expansions
- New Markets

---

# Inputs

Competitor analysis begins after receiving:

- Approved Market Research
- Product Idea
- Business Opportunity
- Feature Proposal

---

# Outputs

This SOP produces:

- Competitor Landscape Report
- Competitor Matrix
- Feature Comparison Matrix
- SWOT Analysis
- Pricing Analysis
- Positioning Analysis
- Opportunity Report
- Strategic Recommendations

All outputs become organizational knowledge.

# Workflow

## Phase 1 — Competitor Identification

### Objective

Identify every relevant competitor.

### Activities

Classify competitors as:

- Direct Competitors
- Indirect Competitors
- Emerging Competitors
- Substitute Solutions
- Enterprise Alternatives

### Deliverable

Competitor Inventory

---

## Phase 2 — Company Profiling

### Objective

Understand each competitor as a business.

### Activities

Collect:

- Company Background
- Founding Year
- Headquarters
- Funding
- Team Size
- Target Customers
- Business Model

### Deliverable

Competitor Profile

---

## Phase 3 — Product Analysis

### Objective

Understand competing products.

### Activities

Document:

- Product Portfolio
- Core Features
- Premium Features
- Integrations
- Platform Support
- User Experience
- AI Capabilities

### Deliverable

Product Comparison Matrix

---

## Phase 4 — Pricing Analysis

### Objective

Evaluate commercial positioning.

### Activities

Compare:

- Pricing Models
- Subscription Plans
- Free Tier
- Enterprise Pricing
- Licensing Model
- Trial Periods

### Deliverable

Pricing Comparison Report

---

## Phase 5 — Customer Analysis

### Objective

Understand customer perception.

### Activities

Research:

- Reviews
- Ratings
- Complaints
- Frequently Requested Features
- User Satisfaction
- Customer Expectations

### Deliverable

Customer Feedback Report

---

## Phase 6 — Technology Assessment

### Objective

Understand technical capabilities.

### Activities

Evaluate:

- Technology Stack
- Performance
- Scalability
- Security
- API Availability
- AI Features
- Integrations

### Deliverable

Technology Assessment

---

## Phase 7 — SWOT Analysis

### Objective

Evaluate strategic position.

Assess:

- Strengths
- Weaknesses
- Opportunities
- Threats

For every major competitor.

### Deliverable

SWOT Matrix

---

## Phase 8 — Gap Analysis

### Objective

Identify opportunities.

Evaluate:

- Missing Features
- Poor User Experience
- Weak Integrations
- High Pricing
- Customer Frustrations
- Untapped Markets

### Deliverable

Gap Analysis Report

---

## Phase 9 — Strategic Positioning

### Objective

Determine ForgeOS positioning.

Answer:

- Why will customers choose us?
- What differentiates us?
- Which problems remain unsolved?
- Where can we outperform competitors?

### Deliverable

Competitive Positioning Report

---

## Phase 10 — Final Recommendation

Provide one recommendation.

Possible outcomes:

- Compete
- Differentiate
- Pivot
- Avoid Market
- Continue Research

Every recommendation must include supporting evidence.

---

# Mandatory Research Questions

Every competitor analysis must answer:

1. Who are the market leaders?
2. Who are the fastest-growing competitors?
3. What problems do they solve?
4. What do customers like?
5. What do customers dislike?
6. Where are competitors weak?
7. Where are competitors strong?
8. What opportunities remain?
9. How can ForgeOS differentiate?
10. Is the market worth entering?

- Competitive landscape is understood

- Strategic risks are documented

- Organizational knowledge increases

- Feature Coverage

# Exit Criteria

Analysis is complete when:

- Final report approved
- Product Department accepts findings
- Knowledge archived
- Strategic recommendation documented

# Architect Notes

Competitor analysis exists to create competitive advantage—not imitation.

ForgeOS studies competitors to discover opportunities, not to replicate products.
