# User Research Standard Operating Procedure (SOP)

## Executive Summary

This Standard Operating Procedure defines how ForgeOS discovers, validates, and documents user needs before any product or feature enters planning and design.

User Research ensures that decisions are driven by real customer behavior rather than assumptions.

Every product initiative shall understand its users before proposing solutions.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of User Research is to understand:

- Who the users are.
- What they are trying to accomplish.
- Which problems they experience.
- How they currently solve those problems.
- Which opportunities exist to improve their experience.

---

# Scope

This SOP applies to:

- New Products
- Product Features
- Mobile Applications
- SaaS Platforms
- AI Products
- UX Improvements
- Existing Product Enhancements

---

# Inputs

Research begins after:

- Market Research approval
- Competitor Analysis completion
- Product discovery request
- Executive initiative

---

# Outputs

This SOP produces:

- User Research Report
- User Personas
- Journey Maps
- Empathy Maps
- Jobs-To-Be-Done Analysis
- Pain Point Analysis
- User Needs Matrix
- Opportunity Report
- Research Recommendation

All outputs become organizational knowledge.

# Workflow

## Phase 1 — Research Planning

### Objective

Define research goals.

### Activities

- Define research objectives
- Identify target audience
- Select research methods
- Define success criteria

### Deliverable

Research Plan

---

## Phase 2 — User Segmentation

### Objective

Identify user groups.

### Activities

Classify users by:

- Demographics
- Experience Level
- Industry
- Job Role
- Goals
- Behaviors

### Deliverable

User Segmentation Report

---

## Phase 3 — Persona Development

### Objective

Create representative user profiles.

### Activities

Document:

- Background
- Goals
- Motivations
- Pain Points
- Frustrations
- Technical Skills
- Success Criteria

### Deliverable

User Persona Document

---

## Phase 4 — User Interviews

### Objective

Understand user behavior.

### Activities

Conduct structured interviews.

Focus on:

- Current workflow
- Existing tools
- Challenges
- Decision process
- Expectations

### Deliverable

Interview Summary

---

## Phase 5 — Journey Mapping

### Objective

Visualize the user experience.

### Activities

Map:

- Entry Point
- Actions
- Decisions
- Emotions
- Obstacles
- Exit Point

### Deliverable

User Journey Map

---

## Phase 6 — Pain Point Analysis

### Objective

Identify recurring problems.

### Activities

Categorize:

- Functional Problems
- Emotional Problems
- Technical Problems
- Financial Problems
- Process Problems

### Deliverable

Pain Point Matrix

---

## Phase 7 — Jobs-To-Be-Done Analysis

### Objective

Understand why users seek a solution.

### Activities

Identify:

- Functional Jobs
- Emotional Jobs
- Social Jobs

### Deliverable

JTBD Report

---

## Phase 8 — Opportunity Identification

### Objective

Transform insights into opportunities.

Evaluate:

- Unmet Needs
- Feature Opportunities
- UX Improvements
- Automation Opportunities
- AI Opportunities

### Deliverable

Opportunity Report

---

## Phase 9 — Validation

### Objective

Validate findings.

### Activities

- Cross-check findings
- Remove assumptions
- Confirm recurring patterns
- Verify conclusions

### Deliverable

Validated Findings

---

## Phase 10 — Final Recommendation

Provide one recommendation.

Possible outcomes:

- Proceed
- Validate Further
- Redesign Concept
- Reject Idea

Every recommendation shall include supporting evidence.

---

# Mandatory Research Questions

Every user research must answer:

1. Who is the user?
2. What problem are they trying to solve?
3. Why is the problem important?
4. How is it solved today?
5. What frustrates users most?
6. What outcomes do users expect?
7. Which needs remain unmet?
8. Which opportunities create the most value?
9. Which assumptions were validated?
10. Should the product proceed?

# Success Criteria

Research is successful when:

- Users are clearly understood
- Product assumptions are validated
- User needs are documented
- UX risks are identified
- Product direction becomes evidence-based

---

# KPIs

The department measures:

- Persona Accuracy
- Research Coverage
- Validation Rate
- Stakeholder Satisfaction
- Product Adoption Correlation
- Knowledge Reuse

---

# Exit Criteria

The SOP is complete when:

- Research approved
- Personas finalized
- Findings documented
- Product Department accepts outputs
- Knowledge archived

---

# Architect Notes

User Research focuses on understanding people—not designing interfaces.

Design begins only after users have been thoroughly understood.

---

# Implementation Notes

Future UX Researchers and AI Professionals shall execute user research according to this procedure.

Alternative research techniques may be introduced without changing the overall lifecycle.

---

# AI Consumption Notes

AI Professionals should prioritize:

- Active listening
- Behavioral analysis
- Evidence-based conclusions
- Empathy
- Pattern recognition

Avoid generating fictional user behavior without supporting evidence.
