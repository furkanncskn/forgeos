# Market Research Standard Operating Procedure (SOP)

## Executive Summary

This SOP defines the official market research process used by ForgeOS.

Every new product idea, feature request, startup concept, SaaS initiative, or innovation project shall begin with this procedure.

The objective is to replace assumptions with evidence before product planning begins.

---

# Purpose

This procedure standardizes market research activities across ForgeOS.

It ensures every project begins with sufficient understanding of:

- Market demand
- Customer problems
- Existing solutions
- Competitive landscape
- Business opportunity

---

# Scope

Applies to:

- New Products
- SaaS Applications
- Mobile Applications
- AI Products
- Internal Tools
- New Features
- Business Ideas

---

# Inputs

Research begins after receiving one or more of the following:

- Product Idea
- Business Opportunity
- Feature Proposal
- Customer Request
- Executive Initiative
- Innovation Proposal

---

# Outputs

This SOP produces:

- Market Research Report
- Market Size Analysis
- Customer Segmentation
- Competitor Overview
- Opportunity Score
- Risk Assessment
- Research Recommendation

These outputs become organizational knowledge.

---

# Roles

| Role | Responsibility |
|------|----------------|
| Chief Research Officer | Final approval |
| Market Research Specialist | Execute research |
| Business Analyst | Analyze opportunity |
| Data Analyst | Validate data |
| Product Department | Consume outputs |

---

# Workflow

## Phase 1 — Research Initiation

### Objective

Understand what will be researched.

### Activities

- Read research request
- Define objectives
- Clarify assumptions
- Identify stakeholders

### Deliverable

Research Brief

---

## Phase 2 — Market Definition

### Objective

Clearly define the market.

### Activities

- Identify industry
- Define target market
- Define geography
- Define customer segment
- Define business model

### Deliverable

Market Definition

---

## Phase 3 — Market Size Analysis

### Objective

Estimate opportunity.

### Activities

Calculate:

- TAM
- SAM
- SOM

Estimate:

- Growth Rate
- Market Maturity
- Demand Trend

### Deliverable

Market Size Report

---

## Phase 4 — Customer Research

### Objective

Understand customers.

### Activities

Identify:

- Customer Personas
- Pain Points
- Needs
- Buying Behavior
- Expectations
- Existing Frustrations

### Deliverable

Customer Analysis Report

---

## Phase 5 — Competitor Research

### Objective

Understand competition.

### Activities

Identify:

- Direct Competitors
- Indirect Competitors
- Market Leaders
- Pricing
- Features
- Business Models
- Strengths
- Weaknesses

### Deliverable

Competitor Analysis

---

## Phase 6 — Trend Analysis

### Objective

Identify future opportunities.

### Activities

Research:

- Technology Trends
- AI Trends
- Regulatory Changes
- Consumer Trends
- Economic Trends

### Deliverable

Trend Report

---

## Phase 7 — Opportunity Assessment

### Objective

Evaluate business potential.

Evaluate:

- Market Opportunity
- Competitive Intensity
- Revenue Potential
- Differentiation
- Scalability
- Risks

### Deliverable

Opportunity Scorecard

---

## Phase 8 — Risk Assessment

### Objective

Identify business risks.

Assess:

- Market Risk
- Financial Risk
- Technical Risk
- Adoption Risk
- Legal Risk
- Competitive Risk

### Deliverable

Risk Report

---

## Phase 9 — Recommendation

Research concludes with one recommendation.

Possible outcomes:

- Proceed
- Proceed with Conditions
- Re-Research
- Reject

Recommendation must include supporting evidence.

---

## Phase 10 — Knowledge Capture

All research artifacts are stored inside ForgeOS Knowledge Base.

Artifacts include:

- Reports
- Sources
- Findings
- Metrics
- Lessons Learned

Knowledge becomes reusable.

---

# Mandatory Research Questions

Every research must answer:

1. What problem exists?

2. Who experiences it?

3. How is it solved today?

4. Who are the competitors?

5. How large is the market?

6. Why will customers switch?

7. What makes our solution unique?

8. What are the risks?

9. Is the opportunity scalable?

10. Should ForgeOS continue?

---

# Required Deliverables

Every Market Research must include:

- Executive Summary
- Problem Statement
- Market Definition
- Customer Analysis
- Competitor Analysis
- Market Size
- Trends
- SWOT Analysis
- Risk Assessment
- Final Recommendation

---

# Quality Gates

Before approval, verify:

☐ Evidence supports conclusions

☐ Sources are cited

☐ Competitors identified

☐ Personas documented

☐ Risks evaluated

☐ Recommendation justified

☐ Documentation complete

---

# Success Criteria

Research is considered successful when:

- Decision uncertainty decreases
- Product team has sufficient evidence
- Market opportunity is quantified
- Risks are documented
- Organizational knowledge increases

---

# KPIs

The department measures:

- Research Completion Time
- Research Accuracy
- Evidence Coverage
- Recommendation Acceptance Rate
- Knowledge Reuse Rate
- Stakeholder Satisfaction

---

# Exit Criteria

The SOP is complete only when:

- Research approved
- Deliverables reviewed
- Knowledge archived
- Product Department accepts outputs

---

# Architect Notes

This SOP intentionally separates research from product planning.

Research discovers reality.

Product defines solutions.

Engineering builds solutions.

---

# Implementation Notes

Future AI Professionals executing market research shall follow this SOP without modification unless superseded by Governance.

---

# AI Consumption Notes

AI Professionals must prioritize:

- Objectivity
- Evidence
- Source credibility
- Quantitative analysis
- Repeatability

Opinions shall never replace validated research.
