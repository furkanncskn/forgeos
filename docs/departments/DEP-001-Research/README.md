# Research Department

> Every successful product begins with understanding.

---

# Executive Summary

The Research Department is responsible for discovering truth before execution.

Its purpose is to eliminate uncertainty by transforming assumptions into validated knowledge.

Every project within ForgeOS begins in the Research Department.

No product, feature, workflow, or implementation should begin without sufficient research.

---

# Department Mission

Provide accurate, structured, evidence-based knowledge that enables every other department to make informed decisions.

---

# Vision

Become the organizational source of truth for market intelligence, user understanding, competitive awareness, and business opportunities.

---

# Department Responsibilities

The Research Department is responsible for:

- Market Research
- Competitor Analysis
- User Research
- Industry Research
- Technology Research
- Trend Analysis
- Opportunity Discovery
- Problem Validation
- Requirement Discovery
- Risk Identification
- Data Collection
- Evidence Gathering

---

# Scope

The department owns every activity performed before product planning begins.

Research is responsible for understanding reality—not creating solutions.

---

# Inputs

Typical inputs include:

- Business Ideas
- Customer Requests
- Product Hypotheses
- Executive Initiatives
- Innovation Proposals
- Strategic Questions

---

# Outputs

The department produces standardized organizational artifacts.

Examples include:

- Market Research Report
- Competitor Analysis Report
- User Persona Report
- Industry Report
- Opportunity Assessment
- SWOT Analysis
- PESTLE Analysis
- Business Problem Definition
- Research Summary
- Recommendation Report

Outputs become organizational knowledge.

---

# Internal Workflow

Every research activity follows the same lifecycle.

```
Research Request

↓

Problem Definition

↓

Research Planning

↓

Data Collection

↓

Analysis

↓

Validation

↓

Documentation

↓

Review

↓

Knowledge Base
```

---

# Downstream Consumers

Research outputs are primarily consumed by:

- Product Department
- Design Department
- Architecture Department
- Marketing Department
- Executive Office

Research never implements solutions.

Research enables better decisions.

---

# Department KPIs

Research performance is measured through:

- Research Accuracy
- Evidence Coverage
- Decision Support Quality
- Documentation Completeness
- Research Reusability
- Stakeholder Satisfaction
- Knowledge Contribution

---

# Quality Gates

Every research deliverable must satisfy the following criteria.

✅ Objective

Research must avoid bias.

---

✅ Traceable

Sources must be identifiable.

---

✅ Evidence-Based

Claims require supporting evidence.

---

✅ Actionable

Research should enable decisions.

---

✅ Reusable

Findings should become organizational knowledge.

---

# Standard Deliverables

Every completed research project should include:

- Executive Summary
- Research Objective
- Scope
- Methodology
- Findings
- Supporting Evidence
- Risks
- Opportunities
- Recommendations
- References

---

# AI Professionals

The Research Department initially consists of the following AI Professionals.

| Role | Responsibility |
|------|----------------|
| Chief Research Officer | Department leadership |
| Market Research Specialist | Market analysis |
| Competitor Analyst | Competitor intelligence |
| User Research Specialist | Customer understanding |
| Technology Research Specialist | Technology trends |
| Business Analyst | Business problem analysis |
| Data Analyst | Quantitative analysis |

Additional AI Professionals may be introduced through governance approval.

---

# Department Standards

The Research Department follows these principles.

- Facts over assumptions.
- Evidence over opinions.
- Research before execution.
- Transparency in methodology.
- Repeatability.
- Source credibility.
- Continuous knowledge accumulation.

---

The Research Department collaborates with:

```
CEO Office

↓

Research

↓

Product

↓

Design

↓

Architecture

↓

Engineering

Marketing
```

Research is the first operational department in every product lifecycle.

---

# Success Criteria

The department succeeds when:

- Organizational uncertainty decreases.
- Better decisions are made.
- Risks are identified early.
- Opportunities are discovered.
- Organizational knowledge grows continuously.
- Future research becomes faster through accumulated knowledge.

---

# Architect Notes

The Research Department intentionally avoids implementation responsibilities.

Its value lies in producing trusted organizational knowledge that supports every downstream department.
