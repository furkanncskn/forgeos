# Product Discovery Standard Operating Procedure (SOP)

## Executive Summary

This Standard Operating Procedure defines how ForgeOS transforms validated research into a product opportunity ready for planning and execution.

Product Discovery ensures the organization builds the right product before investing engineering effort.

No PRD may be created without completing this SOP.

---

# Purpose

The objective of Product Discovery is to answer one question:

> **Should ForgeOS build this product?**

This procedure converts research findings into a validated product concept supported by business value, customer value, and technical feasibility.

---

# Scope

Applies to:

- New Products
- SaaS Platforms
- Mobile Applications
- AI Products
- Internal Platforms
- Major Features
- Product Expansions

---

# Inputs

Product Discovery begins only after receiving:

- Approved Market Research
- Approved Competitor Analysis
- Approved User Research
- Technology Research
- Business Objectives

---

# Outputs

This SOP produces:

- Product Discovery Report
- Problem Statement
- Product Vision Draft
- Product Opportunity Assessment
- Value Proposition Canvas
- Success Metrics
- Initial Feature Inventory
- Product Recommendation

These outputs become Product Department assets.

# Workflow

## Phase 1 — Discovery Kickoff

### Objective

Define the discovery initiative.

### Activities

- Review research
- Identify stakeholders
- Define objectives
- Define success criteria

### Deliverable

Discovery Brief

---

## Phase 2 — Problem Validation

### Objective

Verify that the problem is worth solving.

### Activities

Evaluate:

- Problem severity
- Frequency
- Business impact
- User impact
- Existing alternatives

### Deliverable

Validated Problem Statement

---

## Phase 3 — Opportunity Assessment

### Objective

Measure opportunity.

### Activities

Assess:

- Market Potential
- Customer Demand
- Revenue Opportunity
- Competitive Advantage
- Strategic Alignment

### Deliverable

Opportunity Assessment

---

## Phase 4 — Value Proposition

### Objective

Define customer value.

### Activities

Document:

- Customer Jobs
- Pain Relievers
- Gain Creators
- Unique Value Proposition
- Product Positioning

### Deliverable

Value Proposition Canvas

---

## Phase 5 — Product Vision

### Objective

Define long-term direction.

### Activities

Describe:

- Product Purpose
- Target Audience
- Expected Outcomes
- Success Definition
- Strategic Goals

### Deliverable

Product Vision Draft

---

## Phase 6 — Initial Feature Discovery

### Objective

Identify major capabilities.

### Activities

List:

- Core Features
- Supporting Features
- Nice-to-Have Features
- Future Ideas

No prioritization occurs yet.

### Deliverable

Feature Inventory

---

## Phase 7 — Business Validation

### Objective

Validate commercial viability.

### Activities

Review:

- Business Model
- Revenue Potential
- Operational Impact
- Cost Estimate
- Strategic Fit

### Deliverable

Business Validation Report

---

## Phase 8 — Technical Feasibility

### Objective

Verify technical viability.

### Activities

Collaborate with Architecture.

Evaluate:

- Complexity
- Technical Risks
- Required Technologies
- Integration Needs
- Scalability

### Deliverable

Technical Feasibility Report

---

## Phase 9 — Success Definition

### Objective

Define measurable success.

Establish:

- Business KPIs
- Product KPIs
- Adoption Metrics
- User Satisfaction
- Revenue Goals

### Deliverable

Success Metrics

---

## Phase 10 — Discovery Recommendation

### Objective

Produce final decision.

Possible outcomes:

- Build
- Build with Constraints
- Research Further
- Reject

Recommendation must be evidence-based.

---

# Mandatory Discovery Questions

Every Product Discovery must answer:

1. What problem are we solving?

2. Who experiences this problem?

3. Why is this problem important?

4. Why should ForgeOS solve it?

5. Why now?

6. What differentiates our solution?

7. What value will customers receive?

8. Can the organization build it?

9. Can the organization sustain it?

10. Should Product Planning begin?

# Success Criteria

Discovery succeeds when:

- Product direction is clear.
- Customer value is validated.
- Business opportunity is measurable.
- Product risk decreases.
- Executive stakeholders approve progression.

---

# KPIs

Discovery performance is measured through:

- Discovery Cycle Time
- Opportunity Validation Rate
- Executive Approval Rate
- Product Success Correlation
- Requirement Stability
- Product Adoption Correlation

---

# Exit Criteria

Discovery is complete when:

- Executive approval obtained
- Discovery artifacts completed
- Product Vision approved
- Product Planning authorized

---

# Architect Notes

Discovery defines whether a product deserves investment.

Planning defines how that investment will be executed.

These responsibilities remain intentionally separate.

---

# Implementation Notes

Every new ForgeOS product shall complete this SOP before Product Requirements (PRD) creation.

Future AI Product Managers shall execute this process without exception.

---

# AI Consumption Notes

AI Professionals should prioritize:

- Customer outcomes
- Strategic alignment
- Business viability
- Technical realism
- Evidence-based recommendations

Never recommend implementation before discovery is complete.
