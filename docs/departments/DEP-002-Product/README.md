# Product Department

> The Product Department transforms validated knowledge into executable products.

---

# Executive Summary

The Product Department is responsible for transforming research into product strategy.

Research discovers reality.

Product defines direction.

Design creates experiences.

Engineering builds solutions.

The Product Department serves as the bridge between business opportunity and software implementation.

---

# Department Mission

Transform validated customer problems into successful digital products through structured product management, strategic planning, prioritization, and continuous product discovery.

---

# Vision

Build products that customers genuinely need by making every product decision evidence-based, measurable, and strategically aligned.

---

# Department Responsibilities

The Product Department owns:

- Product Discovery
- Product Strategy
- Product Vision
- Product Roadmap
- Product Requirements
- Feature Definition
- Backlog Management
- Prioritization
- Business Rules
- Acceptance Criteria
- Release Planning
- Product Analytics
- Product Success Measurement

---

# Scope

The department begins its work after Research has completed validation.

The Product Department defines:

- What should be built.
- Why it should be built.
- Who it is for.
- When it should be built.
- How success will be measured.

The Product Department does **not** design interfaces or implement software.

# Department Standards

The Product Department follows these principles:

- Customer Value First
- Evidence Before Decisions
- Simplicity Over Complexity
- Outcomes Over Features
- Prioritization Over Volume
- Documentation Before Development
- Continuous Product Discovery

# Success Criteria

The department succeeds when:

- Products solve validated problems.
- Requirements remain stable.
- Teams build the right features.
- Customer value continuously increases.
- Product decisions remain measurable.
- Organizational knowledge expands.

---

# Architect Notes

The Product Department owns product decisions.

Implementation decisions belong to Engineering.

Technical decisions belong to Architecture.

Design decisions belong to the Design Department.
