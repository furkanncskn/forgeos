# Departments

> Departments are the operational engine of ForgeOS.

---

# Purpose

The **Departments** directory defines every operational department within ForgeOS.

Each department represents a specialized business capability responsible for producing measurable organizational outcomes.

Departments execute strategy.

Governance defines the rules.

Organization defines the structure.

Departments deliver the work.

---

# Objectives

The Departments directory exists to:

- Separate responsibilities into specialized domains.
- Eliminate role ambiguity.
- Standardize operational execution.
- Improve scalability.
- Encourage specialization.
- Enable parallel execution.
- Promote organizational accountability.

---

# Department Philosophy

ForgeOS is not driven by AI models.

ForgeOS is driven by specialized departments working together under a unified operating system.

Every department has:

- A clear mission.
- Defined responsibilities.
- Expected deliverables.
- Standard operating procedures.
- Quality Gates.
- Performance indicators.
- Collaboration rules.

---

# Department Collaboration

Departments do not operate independently.

Every department:

- receives standardized inputs;
- produces standardized outputs;
- hands deliverables to downstream departments;
- provides feedback to upstream departments.

Operational flow is continuous.

---

# Department Ownership

Each department shall have:

- Department Owner
- Department Mission
- Department KPIs
- AI Professionals
- Standard Workflows
- Quality Gates
- Performance Reviews

No department operates without ownership.

---

# Communication Model

Departments communicate through documented artifacts rather than assumptions.

Examples include:

- Research Reports
- Product Requirements
- Design Systems
- Architecture Decisions
- Technical Specifications
- Test Reports
- Release Notes
- Marketing Plans

Every handoff must produce documentation.

---

# Quality Standards

Every department shall:

- Follow Governance.
- Follow Operating Principles.
- Produce reviewable outputs.
- Preserve organizational knowledge.
- Participate in continuous improvement.

Departments are evaluated on consistency rather than speed alone.

---

# Department Independence

Departments are autonomous within their defined responsibilities.

Cross-department decisions require documented collaboration.

Strategic conflicts shall be escalated according to the Governance Model.

# Architect Notes

Departments are organizational capabilities rather than technical modules.

Their purpose is to organize work into predictable, reusable, and measurable business functions.

---

# Implementation Notes

Future AI Professionals, workflows, templates, and operating procedures shall be assigned to departments rather than existing independently.

Every operational activity must belong to exactly one primary department.

---

# AI Consumption Notes

AI Professionals should determine:

- which department owns the task;
- what inputs are required;
- what outputs must be produced;
- which downstream department receives the deliverable.

Department boundaries should always be respected.
