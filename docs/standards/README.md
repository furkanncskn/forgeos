# Enterprise Standards

## Overview

The **Enterprise Standards** department defines the universal principles, governance rules and cross-functional standards that apply across every ForgeOS department.

Unlike domain-specific standards, Enterprise Standards establish organization-wide rules that ensure consistency, interoperability, governance, quality and long-term sustainability.

Every department shall comply with these standards unless an approved exception has been documented.

---

# Objectives

The Enterprise Standards aim to:

- Establish enterprise-wide governance
- Standardize engineering practices
- Improve interoperability
- Strengthen operational consistency
- Support regulatory compliance
- Reduce organizational complexity
- Enable AI-assisted engineering
- Increase enterprise scalability
- Improve documentation quality
- Build a unified engineering culture

# Governance

Enterprise Standards operate under the authority of the **Enterprise Architecture Board (EAB)** together with the **Executive Technology Council (ETC)**.

All enterprise standards shall define:

- Owner
- Reviewers
- Version
- Status
- Metadata
- Review Schedule
- Approval History
- Related Standards

Exceptions require documented business justification, risk assessment, compensating controls and an expiration date.
