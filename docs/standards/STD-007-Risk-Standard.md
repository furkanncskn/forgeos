# Risk Standard

## Executive Summary

Risk management enables the organization to proactively identify, evaluate, mitigate and continuously monitor threats that may impact business objectives, technology platforms and operational resilience.

This standard defines the enterprise framework for managing strategic, operational, technical, cybersecurity, compliance and AI-related risks throughout the ForgeOS ecosystem.

ForgeOS adopts a **Risk-Based Decision Model**, ensuring that governance, investment and operational priorities are aligned with measurable risk.

---

# Purpose

This standard aims to:

- Standardize enterprise risk management
- Reduce operational uncertainty
- Improve governance
- Strengthen decision making
- Support regulatory compliance
- Increase organizational resilience
- Improve AI governance
- Enable continuous risk monitoring

# Guiding Principles

## Risk is Continuous

Risk management is an ongoing process throughout the lifecycle of every enterprise initiative.

---

## Business Driven

Risk decisions shall balance business value, cost and organizational objectives.

---

## Evidence-Based Assessment

Risk evaluation shall rely on measurable evidence whenever possible.

---

## Shared Responsibility

Every department is responsible for identifying and managing risks within its area of responsibility.

---

## Continuous Improvement

Risk controls shall evolve in response to organizational learning, emerging threats and technology changes.

# Risk Assessment

Risk evaluation shall consider:

- Business Impact
- Financial Impact
- Operational Impact
- Customer Impact
- Security Impact
- Compliance Impact
- Recovery Complexity
- Reputational Impact

Risk assessments shall use approved enterprise methodologies.

---

# Risk Scoring

Recommended scoring model:

```
Risk Score = Likelihood × Impact
```

Example scale:

| Score | Classification |
|-------:|----------------|
| 1–4 | Low |
| 5–9 | Medium |
| 10–16 | High |
| 17–25 | Critical |

Organizations may customize thresholds while maintaining governance consistency.

---

# Risk Treatment

Accepted treatment strategies include:

- Avoid
- Mitigate
- Transfer
- Accept

Treatment decisions shall include documented justification.

---

# Risk Register

The enterprise risk register shall include:

- Risk Identifier
- Description
- Category
- Owner
- Likelihood
- Impact
- Score
- Mitigation Plan
- Review Date
- Status

Risk registers shall remain continuously updated.

---

# Risk Monitoring

Continuous monitoring shall include:

- Open Risks
- Critical Risks
- Emerging Risks
- Mitigation Progress
- Residual Risk
- Control Effectiveness
- Trend Analysis

Enterprise dashboards shall provide executive visibility.

---

# Residual Risk

Residual risk shall be evaluated after mitigation activities.

Residual risk exceeding enterprise tolerance shall require executive approval.

---

# Risk Reporting

Reports shall include:

- Current Risk Profile
- Top Enterprise Risks
- Mitigation Status
- Trends
- KPI Performance
- Control Effectiveness
- Emerging Threats

Risk reporting shall support operational and executive decision making.

---
