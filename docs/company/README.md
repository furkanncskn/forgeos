# Company

> ForgeOS is an AI-native software company built as an operating system rather than a collection of tools.

---

# Purpose

The **Company** directory defines the identity of ForgeOS.

While the **Governance** directory defines how the organization is governed, the **Company** directory defines who ForgeOS is.

It contains the organization's identity, philosophy, culture, communication style, branding, long-term positioning, and public-facing company information.

---

# Objectives

The Company directory exists to:

- Define the identity of ForgeOS.
- Preserve organizational culture.
- Standardize company communication.
- Establish long-term positioning.
- Provide a single source of truth for company information.
- Ensure every department communicates with a unified voice.

---

# Scope

The Company directory contains documents that describe ForgeOS as an organization rather than as a technical system.

Typical documents include:

- Mission
- Vision
- Brand Identity
- Company Culture
- Company Story
- Communication Style
- Public Positioning
- Company Principles

---

# Relationship with Governance

Governance answers:

> **"How does ForgeOS operate?"**

Company answers:

> **"Who is ForgeOS?"**

Governance defines authority.

Company defines identity.

---

# Organizational Responsibility

The Company directory is maintained by the CEO Office.

Every document within this directory shall:

- align with Governance;
- reflect the Mission;
- support the Vision;
- reinforce the Core Values.

---

# Writing Standards

Every Company document must be:

- Human-readable
- Professionally written
- Technology independent
- Consistent with company identity
- Suitable for both humans and AI Professionals

---

# Audience

The Company directory is intended for:

- Human contributors
- AI Professionals
- Business stakeholders
- Partners
- Future employees
- Open-source contributors

---

# Reading Order

New contributors should read:

1. README (this document)
2. Mission
3. Vision
4. Core Values
5. Brand Identity
6. Company Culture

---

# Architect Notes

The Company directory intentionally avoids technical implementation.

Its responsibility is to preserve organizational identity.

---

# Implementation Notes

Future departments, AI Professionals, and public documentation should reference Company documents to ensure consistent communication.

---

# AI Consumption Notes

AI Professionals should use the Company directory to understand the identity, tone, philosophy, and communication style of ForgeOS.

When generating public-facing content, Company documents take precedence over implementation documents.
